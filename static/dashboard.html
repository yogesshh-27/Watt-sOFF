Theft Prevention Architecture

How Watt's Off Stops Power Theft

#01

Under-Consumption Isolation

Unlike naive spike monitors, our engine detects negative baseline deviations — identifying bypassed meters recording artificially diminished units.

#02

Hardware Tamper Sensors

Direct telemetry feeds from optical terminal cover switches, neodymium magnetic field hall sensors, and neutral loop bypass circuits.

#03

Transformer Energy Balances

Automated non-technical loss accounting flags distribution transformers delivering higher kilowatt-hours than the aggregate consumer billing sum.

#04

Non-Theft Over-Consumption Guard

High usage anomalies are strictly categorized as load surges (0 theft score), preventing costly false accusations and protecting honest consumers.
