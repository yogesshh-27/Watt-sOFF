Statutory Legal Protections & Whistleblower Immunity

Reporting power theft is an official civic duty protected under Indian Central Electricity legislation. Read your statutory rights and how your privacy is safeguarded.

Section 135 — Theft of Electricity

Defines criminal penalties up to 3 years imprisonment and compounding fines up to three times the financial damage for any direct tapping, tampering, or unauthorized diversion.

Section 150 — Whistleblower Immunity

Provides legal protection ensuring informants cannot be subjected to harassment, defamation suits, or retaliatory complaints by apprehended theft offenders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the person I report ever know my identity? ▼
Absolutely not. Under DISCOM Vigilance SOP, raid orders provided to enforcement officers and police teams redact all complainant identifiers. If you choose 'Anonymous Mode', no identifying information is even recorded in the raid docket.
How is the cash reward calculated and paid? ▼
The reward is fixed at 10% of the compounding penalty realized from the offender (subject to a minimum of ₹5,000 and maximum of ₹50,000 per case). The bounty is credited directly to your bank account or verified UPI ID within 14 business days of penalty recovery.
What kind of photo evidence gives the highest priority? ▼
Clear, daylight or illuminated photos showing: (1) The hooked wire connecting to overhead mains, (2) The meter terminal or bypassed service cable, (3) A nearby landmark, shop signage, or electric pole number. Geotagged images enable dispatch within 2 hours.
What happens if I make an honest mistake about a location? ▼
Citizen reports are always cross-checked with smart meter telemetry and distribution transformer energy balances before raid teams are sent. You are protected from any liability for honest reports made in good faith.
